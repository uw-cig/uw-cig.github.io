## Course Description

Computational imaging studies how computation and mathematical modeling can be used to form images from indirect, incomplete, or noisy measurements. This course introduces the mathematical and computational foundations of modern computational imaging, with an emphasis on inverse problems and reconstruction algorithms.

## Lecture Topics

1. Intro to Computational Imaging
2. Images as Functions and Vectors
3. Image Restoration (Denoising and Inpainting)
4. Image Restoration (Deblurring and Super-resolution)
5. Image Reconstruction (CT)
6. Image Reconstruction (MRI)
7. Ill-Posedness and Regularization
8. Bayesian Inverse Problems
9. Smooth Optimization
10. Nonsmooth Optimization
11. Proximal Gradient Method
12. Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers
13. Sparse Models and Compressed Sensing
14. Total Variation Regularization
