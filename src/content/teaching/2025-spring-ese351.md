## Course Description

Signals and systems studies how signals represent information and how systems process, transform, and transmit them. This course introduces the mathematical and computational foundations of signal analysis and linear systems, with an emphasis on time- and frequency-domain representations, convolution, Fourier analysis, and system properties.
