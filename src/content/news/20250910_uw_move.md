After 8 years at [WashU ESE/CSE](https://engineering.washu.edu/index.html), Prof. Kamilov and CIG will be moving to [UW-Madison ECE](https://engineering.wisc.edu/departments/electrical-computer-engineering).

Prof. Kamilov looks forward to collaborating with new colleagues at UW and will miss WashU and St. Louis, which hold many great memories.
