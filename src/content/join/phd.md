The **Computational Imaging Group (CIG)** at the University of Wisconsin–Madison is recruiting a PhD student for **Fall 2027**. Our current research interests include **generative models, self-supervised learning, optimization, and large-scale inference**, with applications to computational imaging and inverse problems. We combine physical models with machine learning to reconstruct and understand the physical world from incomplete and noisy measurements.

CIG recruits students through both **Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)** and **Computer Sciences (CS)**. Applicants should apply through the corresponding graduate program and mention **Ulugbek Kamilov** in their statement of purpose.

- [ECE PhD program and application information](https://engineering.wisc.edu/programs/degrees/electrical-and-computer-engineering-phd/)
- [CS graduate admissions and application information](https://www.cs.wisc.edu/grad/programs/admissions/)
